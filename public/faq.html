Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for families, brands, and private hosts planning refined celebrations with The Royal Velvet.

What does The Royal Velvet specialize in?

The Royal Velvet specializes in luxury weddings, destination celebrations, corporate events, private family milestones, cultural ceremonies, decor styling, entertainment, hospitality, and complete event production across India.

Where is The Royal Velvet based?

The Royal Velvet is based in HSR Layout, Bangalore, Karnataka, and serves clients across Bangalore and major Indian destinations.

Do you plan luxury weddings in Bangalore?

Yes. We plan premium weddings in Bangalore with concept design, ritual planning, decor, hospitality, logistics, entertainment, photography coordination, and execution.

Do you handle destination weddings?

Yes. We support destination weddings across India with guest travel, venue coordination, decor, rituals, entertainment, hospitality desk, wellness support, and after-event care.

Do you manage corporate events?

Yes. We curate product launches, annual days, executive gatherings, award functions, employee celebrations, off-sites, and brand-led corporate experiences.

Can you manage cultural and traditional ceremonies?

Yes. We handle poojas, housewarming ceremonies, Varalakshmi pooja, Sathyanarayana pooja, Ganesh pooja, Diksha ceremonies, Dhoti ceremonies, half-saree ceremonies, Shashti Poorthi, and ear piercing events.

Can we customize a package?

Yes. Clients can begin with a package and add custom services such as celebrity management, choreography, travel, invites, hospitality, decor upgrades, camera crews, drone visuals, wellness, or elder and child care.

Do you provide end-to-end event management?

Yes. We can manage the complete flow from consultation, concept, planning, vendor coordination, budgeting support, design, guest logistics, on-ground execution, and final handover.

Do you provide decor and floral styling?

Yes. We design luxury floral settings, mandapams, stage decor, tablescapes, lighting moods, entry concepts, ceremonial backdrops, and premium event styling.

Do you arrange artists and entertainment?

Yes. We can coordinate hosts, DJs, live music, vadhyam, drums, classical ensembles, choreographers, celebrity appearances, and curated entertainment direction.

Do you coordinate photography and reels?

Yes. We can coordinate photography, cinematography, drone coverage, reel-ready moments, stage framing, entry captures, and complete visual production support.

What type of clients do you serve?

We serve families, entrepreneurs, premium brands, corporate teams, private hosts, and clients who want a polished, discreet, and elevated celebration experience.

How early should we book?

For large weddings and destination events, early consultation is recommended so venues, premium vendors, artists, and design production can be secured with care. Smaller events can be discussed based on availability.

Do you plan events across India?

Yes. The Royal Velvet supports celebrations across India and plans each occasion around the client location, venue, guest count, rituals, and production requirements.

Do you offer membership or preferred pricing?

Membership and preferred pricing may be available for corporate clients, returning families, and annual celebration partners, subject to event scale and package selection.

Can you manage guest hospitality?

Yes. We can support welcome desks, guest movement, accommodation coordination, elder care, child care, travel flow, gifting, and private hospitality details.

Can you handle religious rituals respectfully?

Yes. Ritual-sensitive planning is part of our service. We coordinate priest management, timings, materials, seating, decor, and guest flow with cultural respect.

How is pricing decided?

Pricing depends on event type, guest count, city, venue, design scale, vendor requirements, production complexity, hospitality depth, and selected services. A private consultation helps define scope.

How do we book a consultation?

You can use the Book Consultation form on the website, call the team, or contact The Royal Velvet through WhatsApp to begin a private event discussion.

Is client information kept private?

Yes. Client information, event plans, personal details, and private celebration requirements are handled with discretion and shared only with authorized planning or vendor partners where required.
